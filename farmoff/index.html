A Twelve Doors game

FarmOff!

Grow it. Ship it. Win the market! A quick, friendly farming race, out now on Android and iPhone: play a season solo, challenge a friend to see who earns the most, or build up your own Zen farm with no clock at all.

Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

New to the game? You'll be farming within seconds, and the Help button can coach you through every job. Or watch the 2-minute how-to-play guide on YouTube.

What you do

Busy hands, short seasons

Every season is a race against the clock, the weather and the critters, with a market that pays less for the crops everyone is selling.

⏱️Short seasons

Play a quick four-minute season or a full six-minute one. Crops ripen in about a minute, so there's always something to plant, water or harvest.

🤝Challenge a friend

Host a game, send your friend the invite code, and farm side by side. Same market, same weather: the best farmer wins.

🧑‍🌾27 farmers, 6 pets

Each farmer has a strength and a weakness. Earn Golden Seeds every season to unlock new ones. A puppy keeps you company from the start, and your first medals win a kitten, a duckling, even a baby dragon.

🍅Six crops

Start with wheat and tomatoes, then unlock peppers, strawberries, coffee and cocoa: bigger rewards, bigger risks.

🐀Pests and helpers

Rats and roaches want your harvest; wasps want you. Shoo them, spray them, net your crops, put up a scarecrow, or set a sprinkler to water four plots at once.

🧭Learn by doing

You'll have your hands in the soil within seconds, with no long tutorial. Stuck? The Help button brings in a coach who walks you through every job, one tap at a time.

🏅Medals and villagers

Every haypence you earn counts toward a medal: Bronze at 180, Silver at 320, Gold at 600. Villagers drop by with orders that pay better than the market.

📅Daily Farm Orders

Three new goals every day for Golden Seeds, with a bonus for finishing all three and a streak to keep going. Festival orders join in for holidays around the world.

⚖️Fair play

Free to play, with no loot boxes and nothing that buys a win.

Zen mode

No clock, and a farm that keeps growing

Want to slow down? Zen mode is your own saved farm, tended at your own pace.

🐄Hens and cows

Keep hens in the coop and cows in the barn, then sell the eggs and milk to villagers or send them off in the truck.

🍞The Farm Kitchen

Build a kitchen and cook bread, jam, cheese and cake from your own harvest. Dishes sell for about twice as much, and villagers ask for them too.

🛠️Farm upgrades

Spend your haypence on a deep well, a market stall, a faster truck, a compost heap, a cosy hen house and a dairy shed. Upgrades stay with your farm.

🏆Milestones and leaders

Chase 35 Zen Milestones for Golden Seeds, pick a farm name, and climb the Zen leaderboard's top 20.
