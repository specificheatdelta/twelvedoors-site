FarmOff!

Grow it. Ship it. Win the market! A quick, friendly farming race for Android: play a season solo, or challenge a friend to see who earns the most.

New to the game? Your first season is a guided lesson, step by step. Or watch the 2-minute how-to-play guide on YouTube.

What you do

Busy hands, short seasons

Every season is a race against the clock, the weather and the critters, with a market that pays less for the crops everyone is selling.

⏱️Short seasons

Play a quick four-minute season or a full six-minute one. Crops ripen in about a minute, so there's always something to plant, water or harvest.

🤝Challenge a friend

Host a game, send your friend the invite code, and farm side by side. Same market, same weather: the best farmer wins.

🧑‍🌾24 farmers, 6 pets

Each farmer has a strength and a weakness. Earn Golden Seeds every season to unlock new ones. A puppy keeps you company from the start, and your first medals win a kitten, a duckling, even a baby dragon.

🍅Six crops

Start with wheat and tomatoes, then unlock peppers, strawberries, coffee and cocoa: bigger rewards, bigger risks.

🐀Pests and helpers

Rats and roaches want your harvest; wasps want you. Shoo them, spray them, net your crops, put up a scarecrow, or set a sprinkler to water four plots at once.

🧭Learn by doing

Your first season walks you through every job one tap at a time: hoe, plant, water, harvest, sell, deal with pests, and shop at the market. Stuck later? The Help button brings the coach back.

🏅Medals and villagers

Every haypence you earn counts toward a medal: Bronze at 180, Silver at 320, Gold at 600. Villagers drop by with orders that pay better than the market.

⚖️Fair play

Free to play, with no loot boxes and nothing that buys a win.
