Our name

Twelve doors, open to every side

A bara-dari is a pavilion built to let air move freely, and its name says how: bara means twelve and dari means doors. Three doorways open in each of its four walls.

On the bank of the Ravi in Lahore stands the Bara-dari of Kamran Mirza, a summer pavilion traditionally dated to 1540 and thought to be the oldest Mughal building in the city. Its doors open in every direction, so the river breeze passes straight through. The river has since changed its course, and today the pavilion stands on an island in the middle of the water.

We loved that idea: a space with no closed walls, where air, water and ideas move in every direction, unhindered. That is how we want to make games, and how we want people to play them.
