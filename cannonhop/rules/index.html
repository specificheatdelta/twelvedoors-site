Cannon Hop Player Rules

Effective: 26 November 2026

Cannon Hop has a few places where other players see what you pick: your name and motto on the TOP 20, TODAY and MY CREW boards, and the crews you start or join. By picking a name in the game you agree to these rules.

The short version: be kind, play fair. There is no tolerance for offensive names or abusive players. We read every report and remove anything that breaks these rules within 24 hours.

1. Names

Your name is 3 to 10 letters or numbers, and the game refuses a list of rude words. A name must not:

  • be rude, sexual, hateful or threatening, or make fun of anyone for who they are;
  • bully, harass or target another player;
  • pretend to be another person, a famous name or Twelve Doors Interactive;
  • share personal details, such as a full real name, phone number or address;
  • advertise anything.

2. Scores

Scores must come from playing the game. Scores from a changed copy of the game, a bot or any other trick are removed.

3. Reporting and blocking

  • Report: tap another player on a board, then Report, and pick a reason. The game opens our feedback form with the details filled in; tap Submit to send it.
  • Block: tap another player on a board, then Block. They disappear from your boards and your crew list straight away. You can undo it in Settings > Blocked players.

4. What we do

We read reports every day. When a name, motto or score breaks these rules, we remove it from the boards within 24 hours of the report. Players who keep breaking the rules have their later entries removed too.

5. Removing your own entry

Leaving your crew in the game removes you from it. To take your name and scores off the boards, email us with your name and we'll remove them.
