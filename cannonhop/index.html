Cannon Hop

Light the fuse. Time the swing. FIRE! A one-tap stunt game, coming soon to Android and iPhone: launch your stuntperson from cannon to cannon, glide across the gaps, and follow a comic-book story from the circus to the clouds.

Coming soon to Google Play Coming soon to the App Store

Your cannon swings by itself: tap at just the right moment to launch, sail through the air and land in the next cannon. Miss, and it's a long way down!

What you do

One tap, a lot of sky

Easy to learn, hard to master: every jump is a single tap, and every landing sets up the next.

🎯One tap to fire

Watch the swing, pick your moment, and fire. Land dead centre for a PERFECT and chain them for big scores.

🪽Wings and flips

Drag to open your wings and glide across the wide gaps. Tap in mid-air to flip for style.

🛝Springboards and balloons

Hit springboards for bonus bounces, and pop balloons on the way for extra points.

📖A comic-book story

Sixteen chapters across four acts, from the Valiant Circus to Rooftop City, Thunder Peaks and the Cloud Kingdom, told in comic pages. Earn stars, find posters and stick the landing in the finish net.

🌬️New tricks every act

Gusts of wind, crumbling ledges, lightning bars, pigeons, rings and bumpers keep every chapter fresh.

🎪Endless mode

Climb as high as you can up the tower, or hop sideways across the sky, then take on the TOP 20 leaderboard.

🧑‍🚀Your stuntperson

Play as a stuntman or a stuntwoman, and dress them up in the dressing room. Pick a stage and the music for your endless runs.

🔓Just play

No account needed: your progress stays on your phone.
