Website privacy

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and it loads nothing from other sites. Every image, video and style comes from this site itself. The only thing ever sent elsewhere is your email address, if you use the free-pages form on the Books page (see below).

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs to keep the service secure. We don't see or use those logs. GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

If you ask for free coloring pages on the Books page, your email address is saved in our Google account (Google Sheets) and used to send you the pages once. If you also tick “tell me when new books come out”, we may email you about new Adam & Botty books. We never share or sell it. To be removed, reply “stop” to any email or write to us.
